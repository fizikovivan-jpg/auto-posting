Data Deletion Instructions — Auto Posting

Last updated: September 24, 2026

If you no longer want Auto Posting to have access to your Facebook or Instagram data, you can revoke the application's access through Meta.

Option 1 — Remove access in Facebook

  1. Open Facebook Settings & Privacy.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Business Integrations / Apps and Websites, depending on the interface shown by Meta.
  4. Find Auto Posting.
  5. Choose Remove / Revoke Access and confirm.

After access is revoked, Auto Posting can no longer use the revoked permissions or access tokens.

Option 2 — Request deletion of stored application data

To request deletion of data stored by Auto Posting, contact the administrator of the Facebook Page or Instagram account that authorized the application and ask for deletion of data associated with that Page/account.

The administrator will remove the applicable stored operational records that are no longer required for the service, subject to any technical or legal retention requirements.

What may be deleted

This can include stored access credentials, Page/account identifiers, publication identifiers, publication history, and analytics records associated with the authorized account.
