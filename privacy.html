Privacy Policy — Auto Posting

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Auto Posting is a private automation application used to publish and manage content on Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts that have explicitly authorized access to the application.

Information we access

Depending on the permissions granted through Meta, Auto Posting may access information required to operate the service, such as Facebook Page identifiers, Instagram account identifiers, access tokens, publication identifiers, content metadata, publishing status, and analytics/insights data made available by the Meta APIs.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested automation features, including publishing content, managing authorized Pages/accounts, checking publication status, and collecting performance analytics where the account owner has granted the required permissions.

Data sharing

Auto Posting does not sell personal information and does not share Meta user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Data storage and security

Access credentials and operational data may be stored on secured infrastructure used to run Auto Posting. Access is limited to the application administrator and is used only for the authorized functionality.

Data retention

Operational data is retained only for as long as it is needed to provide the service or maintain publication history and analytics. Access tokens become unusable after access is revoked or otherwise invalidated by Meta.

Removing access and deleting data

A user can revoke Auto Posting's access at any time through the Facebook/Meta settings for connected apps and business integrations. Instructions for requesting deletion of data associated with Auto Posting are available on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

Third-party services

Auto Posting uses Meta Platforms APIs and is subject to the relevant Facebook and Instagram platform terms and policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

Privacy or deletion requests can be submitted to the administrator of the Facebook Page or Instagram account that authorized Auto Posting.
